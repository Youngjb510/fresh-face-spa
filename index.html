About

Skincare with a gentle hand

Fresh Face Spa is a boutique esthetician studio in San Diego's Mission Valley, owned and run by esthetician Raquel Cartlidge. Every visit blends corrective, results-driven skincare with deeply relaxing technique — clear-skin goals met with slow, calming rituals.

The studio's signature is fascia facial massage: a gentle, sculpting massage that releases tension in the face, neck, and scalp for a softer, more lifted look — and a quieter nervous system.

Focus areas

  • Acne

    Calming, congestion-clearing treatments that respect your barrier.

  • Pigmentation

    Brightening exfoliation and renewal to even out tone.

  • Sensitivity

    Gentle, fragrance-free rituals that soothe reactive skin.

  • Age management

    Firming actives and massage for a rested, lifted look.

Fascia facial massage

A studio signature — ask about adding it to any facial for a lifted, de-tense finish.
